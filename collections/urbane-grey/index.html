Collection Story

THE PHILOSOPHY OF URBANE GREY

URBANE GREY introduces architectural slate aesthetics into modern metropolitan dining, framing colorful sauces and artisanal cuts with sophisticated contrast.

Design Language

Crisp rims, fluid oval contours, structured edge platters, and generous deep coupe profiles.

Material Character

Commercial vitrified porcelain with non-porous mineral glaze resistant to metal marking and chipping.

Glaze & Finish

Mineral slate grey satin matte with subtle textural depth.

Hospitality Positioning

Urban dining, steak & grill rooms, contemporary pasta bars, cocktail lounges, hotel restaurants.

Editorial Campaign
